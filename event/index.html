Water Horizons 2025

Transforming Utilities for a Resilient, Net Zero Future

Water Horizons 2025 is a two-day conference that brings together global leaders in the water sector to explore the latest innovations and strategies for building resilient, sustainable water utilities. With a focus on technology opportunities, digital transformation, and regulatory compliance, this event will provide valuable insights into the future of water management.

What we will cover

Technology Opportunities, Digital Transformation, and the Regulatory Horizon – Day 1 explores how utilities are embracing advanced technologies — from AI and IoT to smart water networks and digital twins — to drive operational efficiency, compliance, and system resilience. With real-world case studies and expert panels, you'll gain insight into how innovation is reshaping utility performance and strategy.

Building Resilient Water Systems & Advancing Net Zero Solutions – Day 2 builds directly on Day 1, shifting the focus to implementation. You’ll explore how utilities are applying innovation to address climate risk, reduce emissions, and move towards circular, low-carbon water systems. From nature-based solutions to adaptive infrastructure and net zero pathways — this is where strategy meets sustainability.

One Event. One Connected Journey. Answering the critical question: How do we future-proof water utilities while advancing climate and carbon goals?

Our Speakers

Patrick Child

Deputy Director-General- (DG ENV), European Commission

Patrick Child is a seasoned diplomat and senior civil servant with extensive experience in European and international affairs. His career in the European Institutions spans over two decades, marked by high-level roles in external relations and policy development.

From January 2010 to December 2011, he served as Director of the External Service at the European Commission’s Directorate-General for External Relations (DG RELEX), overseeing the Commission’s external operations and contributing to the shaping of the EU’s global outreach.

Prior to this, Patrick held prominent positions within the European Commission, including Head of Cabinet for two successive Commissioners for External Relations. From November 2004 to January 2010, he was the Head of Cabinet for Commissioner Benita Ferrero-Waldner, where he played a key role in managing EU foreign policy and the European Neighborhood Policy. He previously served in the same capacity for Commissioner Chris Patten from August 2003 to November 2004, after having been a Member of his Cabinet from 1999.

His expertise in economic and monetary affairs was demonstrated earlier in his career at the European Commission, where he served as Press Spokesman on Economic and Monetary matters from 1995 to 1999, and as Administrator in the Monetary Affairs Directorate from 1994 to 1995.

Peter Vale

Carbon & Circular Economy Architect, Severn Trent, UK

Pete is the Carbon and Circular Economy Architect in Severn Trent’s Innovation Team. He has a degree in Environmental Science from the University of Sheffield and a master’s degree in water and wastewater engineering from Cranfield University.

Pete has worked in R&D and innovation in the water industry for 25 years and has played a key role in developing and implementing Severn Trent’s wastewater treatment strategy. Over the past decade he has worked on nutrient removal and recovery processes, removal of emerging contaminants and low energy processes some of which have now been implemented at full scale.

His current area of focus is on technologies that will deliver low carbon, low energy, resource recovery treatment processes that will help Severn Trent deliver on its commitment to be net zero carbon emitters by 2030. He was the architect of Severn Trent’s ‘Net Zero Hub’ a groundbreaking project utilising novel technologies to monitor and mitigate process emissions from a large urban wastewater treatment plant.

Marcus Rink

Chief Inspector, Drinking Water Inspectorate (Defra, UK)

Mohamed Shameel Malick Basha is a senior utility expert with over 16 years of experience in water and electricity metering, smart grid systems, and digital transformation. He currently leads the Bulk Metering Unit at TAQA Distribution Abu Dhabi, where he focuses on advanced metering infrastructure (AMI), data-driven analytics, and Non-Revenue Water (NRW) reduction strategies.

With a strong background in SAP HANA, GIS, he plays a vital role in optimizing utility operations. Mohamed will speak at the World Utility Congress 2025 in Abu Dhabi, presenting insights on innovative approaches to NRW reduction and smart water management.

Koldo Urkullu

COO, Consorcio de Aguas Bilbao Bizkaia, Spain

Koldo Urkullu is a senior mechanical engineer from the Bilbao School of Engineering with extensive expertise in infrastructure management, energy, and water systems. He is the Director of Operations and Asset Management at the Bilbao Bizkaia Water Consortium (CABB), leading modernization, energy efficiency, and telecommunications projects.

Previously, he was Deputy General Manager at Global Energy Services, overseeing the construction and maintenance of wind and solar farms worldwide. Additionally, he was a professor at the Bilbao School of Industrial Engineering, teaching courses on fluid mechanics and hydraulic system management. His expertise spans corporate strategy, asset optimization, and technological integration.

Katy Bevan

Operations Manager, United Utilities, UK

Katy is a water industry professional with over 10 years of experience at United Utilities, one of the UK’s largest listed water companies.

Strong background in wastewater network operations and asset maintenance programme delivery, with current role as Operations Manager in the newly formed Rainwater Management function at UU with a focus on maintenance strategy creation for our new SuDS (Sustainable Drainage Systems) asset base. Key experience in efficiency challenges and the support of onboarding new technologies into the business, leveraging benefit and promotion of application to a wider audience..

Hamish Todd

R&I Net Zero Senior Programme Manager, Scottish Water UK

Hamish holds a Masters in Chemistry and is a Chartered Scientist. With 17 years’ experience in the water industry ranging from analytical testing, method development, management, international consultancy, laboratory design and now programme manager for research and innovation - net zero, he has a broad understanding of the overall industry and the relationships between the different drivers in water and wastewater management.

Now he has a focus on investigating innovative approaches to tackling process emissions measurement and mitigation, as well as other net zero enabling innovations. He is supporting Scottish Water’s net zero team to reach their strategic target of going beyond net zero by 2040.

Geoff Darch

Head of Supply Demand Strategy, Anglian Water, UK

Geoff is Head of Strategic Asset Planning at Anglian Water, accountable for developing, monitoring and reporting on strategic plans for water and wastewater infrastructure, for related evidence and advice, and for resource trading.

Geoff is also a Non-Executive Director of Water Resources East, the regional, multi-sector membership organisation pioneering new approaches to managing water resources. Prior to joining Anglian Water in 2017, Geoff spent 16 years in consultancy, advising on infrastructure climate risks and adaptation for international finance institutions, government departments, environmental agencies and utility companies.

Geoff has a PhD in hydro-climatology from UEA, is a Chartered Scientist, and a Fellow of CIWEM and the Royal Meteorological Society.

Prof.Andrea Guerrini

Commissioner, ARERA & Vice-President, WAREG (Italy)

Andrea Guerrini is Commissioner of ARERA (2018-2025), the Italian Regulatory Authority for Energy, Networks and Environment, Vice-President of WAREG, the Association of European Water Regulators and Member of the Bureau of the Network of Economic Regulators – NER of the OECD. From 2016 until his appointment in ARERA in August 2018, he was President of ASA S.p.A. the water utility of the province of Livorno.

Mr Guerrini is professor at the Department of Business Administration of the University of Verona, on mandatory leave following his appointment in ARERA. He has been leading research projects financed by the European Union and he was one of the founders of WaMaTeRC, the Water Management and Technology Research Centre.

Professor Guerrini designed the tariff methodology for drinking water services for the Autonomous Province of Bolzano.

Alex Adam

Deputy Director, Strategy & Stewardship, The Rivers Trust, UK

Alex works for The River Trust with responsibility for establishing the Water Stewardship service, which aims to help business and others work collaboratively to deliver sustainable solutions for water at a catchment scale. He is the UK lead for the Courtauld 2030 commitment for collective action on water for the UK food and drink industry and a founding member of the Riverscapes partnership with the Woodland Trust, National Trust and Beavers Trust .
Before joining The River Trust`s he worked at WWF -UK and before that spent 14 years at Defra and Natural England working on, amongst other things, in the design and delivery of agri-environment schemes . His early career was spent in marketing and advertising in the IT and Telecoms sector.

Alex is member of the Catchment Based Approach (CaBA) national support group, an Alliance for Water Stewardship expert practitioner and member of the BSI technical committee for water management, he is also a Town Councillor. He spends most of his spare time trying to keep his two sons exhausted and occasionally gets the chance to play golf or go fly fishing.
